Seaside refund policy

Seaside refunds a new shared or reseller plan within 14 days of the first

invoice if the account holder asks in writing.

After 14 days, prepaid time is not returned. Domain registrations and TLS

certificates are never refundable.

A refund returns the unused prepaid balance as account credit, not to the

original payment method. Hosting stays online until the current period ends

unless the account holder asks for an immediate cancel.
