Seaside TLS certificates

Every hosted name receives a free automatically renewed certificate, including

addon domains and subdomains the panel created.

Wildcard certificates are a paid add-on. Private keys never leave the server.

Seaside does not accept a customer-supplied certificate; a custom certificate

is issued through the panel's SSL section only.
