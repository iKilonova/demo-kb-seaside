Seaside mailbox procedure

Every domain includes five mailboxes. Extra mailboxes are sold in packs of ten.

To add a mailbox, open Email, then Accounts, then Create. Passwords must be at

least 16 characters. The daily send cap is 200 messages per mailbox.

Webmail is at mail. plus the primary domain. IMAP is port 993, SMTP is

port 587 with STARTTLS. The hostname is mail. plus the primary domain for

both.
